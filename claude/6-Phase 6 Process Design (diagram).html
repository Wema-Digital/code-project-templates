The three forks

  1. 01 Git structure for features/ A plain git worktree add against the live coding-project-templates checkout leaves the generated folder secretly tied to that path. The recommendation: clone a bare copy into .git-store/ inside the output first, then add worktrees against that local clone.
  2. 02 Fate of the monolith ProjectSetup-linux-os.py does five unrelated jobs in one file, and its docs generation is already broken. Recommendation: retire it in favour of four single-purpose scripts.
  3. 03 Sequencing against claude-code-advance Whether claude-code-advance needs its own generic agent, subagent, and hook examples built out before vscode-workspace-gen is layered on top of it.
→ Once these three are settled, claude/7-Phase 6 Detailed Breakdown.md and scripts/update-github-project-phase6.sh follow.
